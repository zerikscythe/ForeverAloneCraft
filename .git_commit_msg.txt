bot_expansion phases 1+2: raid pool bots and hostile bot AI

- RaidRequestCommand / RaidDismissCommand added to grammar
- ParseRaidVerb with ClassNameToId helper

  registers ObjectGuid::Empty as sentinel, no position seeding
- CompanionAI null-hardening: LoadCombatDoctrine, GetPreparedCombatProfile,
  TryExecuteProfileRotation all safe with owner=nullptr
- TickHostile: no follow/buffs, picks GetVictim()/attacker, runs full doctrine
- CompanionAIEvent::Execute: ownerless branch on _ownerGuid.IsEmpty()
- ScheduleHostileCompanionAI: schedules event with ObjectGuid::Empty
